SHELF-THOUGHT, INC. — BRAND KIT V2.0
Concept: “Shelf-Thought” is deliberate wordplay on “Self-Taught”.

CORE IDEA
The brand celebrates people who are open-minded, curious and focused on becoming better people than they were yesterday: independent learners, researchers, builders, investigators, creators and curious professionals. 
“Shelf” provides the visual metaphor for accumulated knowledge; 
“Thought” represents the reasoning that metamorphosizes knowledge into original concepts and novel ideas.

PRIMARY TAGLINE
SELF TAUGHT. FREER, BIGGER THINKING.

VOICE
Clear, intelligent, curious, independent, practical, confident without being boastful.

LOGO
The rising book spines represent knowledge accumulated through self-directed learning. 
The shelf line grounds the mark. The upward rhythm suggests growth.

COLORS
Deep Navy  #0B2D4A — credibility, focus, depth
Teal       #00A8A8 — learning, discovery, movement
Amber      #F5A623 — curiosity, creativity, insight
Warm Gray  #F5F4EF — editorial space
Charcoal   #374151 — readable supporting text
White      #FFFFFF — reverse applications

TYPE
Montserrat SemiBold/Bold — headlines, logo-adjacent type, labels.
Inter Regular/Medium — body copy, documents, interfaces.

USAGE
Use the horizontal mark for normal corporate applications.
Use the stacked mark for square/compact placements.
Use the icon alone for favicon, avatar, app icon, watermark and merchandise.

CLEAR SPACE
Keep at least the height of the capital “S” around the logo.
Minimum digital full-logo width: 180 px. Use the icon below that size.

DO NOT
Stretch, rotate, skew, add drop shadows, apply gradients to the logo, recolor outside the approved palette, or put the full-color mark on a busy background.

BRAND LANGUAGE
Useful recurring phrases include:
- Self taught. Freer, bigger thinking.
- Learn your way.
- Think for yourself.
- Knowledge you earn.
- Built by curiosity.
- You learn to become how you think.

Naming architecture ideas (only if they become actual offerings): Shelf Notes, Shelf Confidence, Deep Thought Index, Idea Shelf, Reference Shelf, Thought Stack.
