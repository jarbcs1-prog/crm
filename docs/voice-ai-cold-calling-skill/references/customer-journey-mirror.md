# Customer-journey-mirror reference

Use this optional exercise only with a warm or post-demo caller who wants to discuss the problem and has enough time for a real conversation. It is a reflective aid, not a closing formula.

## Safe sequence

1. Ask how the caller normally chooses a provider or service.
2. Ask what they notice first and what would make them choose another option.
3. Reflect their answer without claiming that every customer behaves identically.
4. Ask whether the caller's own customers have a similar path and what happens when the business is unavailable.
5. Ask what outcome would be useful, rather than telling the caller what they must want.

The caller may disagree with the analogy. Follow their description instead of forcing agreement.

## When to use it

Use it when the caller has already experienced the offer, wants a deeper discovery conversation, and has not asked only for a price or a human.

Do not use it when the call is very short, the caller is price-first, the buyer is technical and debating the analogy, the process is enterprise-complex, or the caller has asked to stop. Do not use it to hide price: answer an explicit price question from the loaded pitch.

## Close behavior

A question is not a promise. After the reflection:

- Ask what, if anything, the caller wants to change.
- Answer concerns with the approved pitch and only the relevant capability.
- Quote price only when the pitch contains it and the caller asked.
- Do not claim a decision, agreement, booking, or guaranteed result.
- Let the caller's next instruction determine whether the call continues or ends.
