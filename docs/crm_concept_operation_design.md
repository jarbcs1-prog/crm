This CRM platform is designed as a commercial product, in other words, it will be sold to whomever will be willing to purchase it and the buying purposes of its buyers are theirs alone. It is due to such reasons that certain phrases from the pitch must be refactored to match the generality of its intended purpose or the variability of the potential pitches that the CRM may end up working with. I made several prior mentions about this particular pitch not having been originally designed for use by AI agents nor that it was going to be used in production releases as the main or the default pitch either. The product is built around the core ideas that:

 A. The CRM is agent-centric and as such, autonomously operated and maintained by its agents; 
 B. That its agents are by default trained in sales and telemarketing and as a result, fully capable of making outbound live calls in the purposes of lead generation and enrichment, starting from just one user prompt, the one that sets their target; 
 C. That the agents' workflow includes OSINT research designed for the same purposes and it's automatically going to apply to any CRM profile entries that are incomplete, incorrect, inaccurate or otherwise missing contact details and other relevant data; 
 D. That the agents are trained and capable of using not just industry standard sales pitches but also custom sales and lead generation/qualification pitches. 
 
 The database and pitch serve the purpose of reinforcing these key ideas while the key ideas rely on the training methods and datasets corpuses used in the process. I'm not trying to advertise this CRM as a "one time payment and off one goes with a fully operational platform". 
 
 My aim is to create return business through custom methods of training, finetuning and adapting AI agents to customers' specific requirements. Customers purchase the system but they are all people and they will all have different preferences, experiences and affinities for different aspects of it, e.g. LLM Providers/Models, VoIP/SIP Providers/Services, CRM Providers/Services, Business Scopes - Customer Service (Outbound/Inbound), Sales and Telemarketing (Outbound/Inbound, many types), Support (also different types) etc. These are aspects that I have no control over and no real way to include in just this one product. 
 
 What I have control over, is product flexibility and adaptability to diverse market segments, primarily its operational efficiency and overall stability/reliability - The idea of one remotely monitored, autonomous, semi-self-reliant telemarketing office, customer service desk, marketing department etc., that can be set up and operational out-of-the-box, in no time and virtually anywhere in the world today without the burden of hundreds of thousands of dollars in expenses before the first order of business is ever discussed. 
 
The real value in my opinion, is going to be accrued via return business and business references: customers will always need custom trained agents to fit the different scopes and purposes of the businesses they're intended for. 

We can both agree that nobody is going to make a killing in this market sector by using medical insurance inbound sales agents as outbound car insurance sales agents or viceversa.
